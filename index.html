Third-Party Advertising – Mintegral SDK

The Application uses the Mintegral advertising SDK to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Mintegral and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain information when advertisements are requested, displayed, or interacted with. Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable laws, this information may include:

  • IP address and general location information.
  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs, where available.
  • Device manufacturer, model, and operating system information.
  • Network connection information.
  • Application and SDK version information.
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and interaction data.
  • Technical information used for diagnostics and fraud prevention.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, attribution, fraud prevention, and advertising optimization.

Some information may be shared with Mintegral and its advertising partners to provide and improve advertising services, in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
